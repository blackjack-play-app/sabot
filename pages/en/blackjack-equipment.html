Cards: 100% plastic if you can

At a blackjack table cards change hands constantly and bend fast. 100% plastic cards last for years and can be washed; paper "linen finish" cards are cheaper but wear out in a few nights.

  • How many decks? 2 to 6 identical decks. With a single deck you reshuffle every two or three rounds.
  • Jumbo index: big numbers are easier to read around a table.
  • Two back colours: shuffle one shoe while the other is in play.

Chips: 300 is enough for 5 players

  • 300-chip case: ideal for 5 or 6 players. 500 for a big table or several tables.
  • Weight: 11 to 14 g clay composite chips feel like a casino; light plastic chips are fine for kids.
  • Colours: 4 or 5 colours are plenty (for example 1, 5, 25, 100, 500).

Dealing shoe and layout

  • Shoe: a 4- to 8-deck shoe makes dealing faster and more authentic. Not needed with 2 decks.
  • Blackjack layout: felt or neoprene, with betting circles and the dealer rule ("stands on 17"). It settles a lot of arguments.
  • Automatic shuffler: handy beyond 4 decks, otherwise optional.

House rules that work

  • Rotate the dealer every 10 rounds so nobody keeps the edge.
  • Simple rules: dealer stands on 17, blackjack pays 3:2, double on two cards, split once.
  • No real money: chips with no value, and a small token prize for the biggest stack.
