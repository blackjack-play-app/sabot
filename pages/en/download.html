Install on Windows

  1. Open {{dl:win:file}}.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. New apps without a paid code-signing certificate get this message.
  3. Choose a folder, click Install. Blackjack Play starts automatically and a desktop shortcut is created.

F11 toggles full screen. Graphics quality adjusts itself; you can change it in Settings → Graphics.

Install on macOS

  1. Open {{dl:mac:file}} and drag Blackjack Play into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click Blackjack Play → Open and confirm. macOS asks this once for apps outside the App Store.
  3. If you see "Blackjack Play is damaged", open Terminal and run xattr -cr "/Applications/Blackjack Play.app", then open it again.

Moving your stats

The website and the app each keep their data on the device. To move it: Settings → Data → Export on the old device, then Import on the new one.

Uninstall

Windows: Settings → Apps → Blackjack Play → Uninstall. macOS: move the app to the Trash. Linux: delete the AppImage, or sudo apt remove blackjack-play.
