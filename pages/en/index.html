Inside the game

Built to make you better, not to make you bet

A table that feels like a table

The dealer pulls cards from the shoe, looks at the hand being played, checks the hole card and sweeps losing bets. Used cards stack up in the discard tray, just like in a casino.

  • Up to 5 hands of your own, with a clear summary of each one
  • Zoom and 5 camera views, plus a locked view made for phones
  • Recorded sounds of real cards and chips, adjustable dealer voice

A coach that explains, not just corrects

Every tip comes with the reason, in plain words: "The dealer shows a 6 and must draw, so he busts often. Don't take any risk."

  • 3 help levels: before every move, feedback after, or none
  • Strategy computed for your table rules (decks, H17, surrender…)
  • Mistakes are logged by situation so you can fix your weak spots

Card counting, for real

AI players fill the table so cards come out at a realistic pace. Every now and then the dealer stops and asks you for the count.

  • 8 systems: Hi-Lo, KO, Hi-Opt I and II, Omega II, Zen, Wong Halves, Red Seven
  • True count from the decks remaining, bet ramp from 1 to 8 units
  • Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 deviations built into the advice

Short drills and a simulator

Got ten minutes? Run a focused drill. Wondering whether a rule really matters? The simulator plays 100,000 rounds in a few seconds.

  • Strategy flash drill against the clock, by hand type
  • Deck countdown and true count conversion
  • Simulator: edge, standard deviation, risk of ruin, bankroll curve

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I win or lose real money?

No. Blackjack Play only uses play chips with no value. There's nothing to buy, bet or cash out. If your stack runs dry, you refill it for free.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in the browser, portrait or landscape. A locked camera view and tap-to-bet chips make it easy with one finger. It's even more comfortable on a computer with keyboard shortcuts (H to hit, S to stand…).

Where are my stats stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can export them to a file and import them on another computer (Settings → Data).

Is card counting legal?

Counting in your head isn't a crime. Casinos can still ask a suspected counter to leave, and using any device at the table is prohibited. Blackjack Play is for understanding the game and practicing, not for gambling.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". What do I do?

That message shows up for new apps without a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Or simply play in your browser, nothing to install.

Which rules are used?

By default: 6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, blackjack pays 3 to 2, double after split, late surrender. Six presets (Las Vegas, Atlantic City, European no-hole-card…) and 16 rules can be changed, and the advice adapts automatically.

The table is open.

The game loads in a few seconds in your browser. Your settings and stats are saved for next time.
