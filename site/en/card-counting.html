How to count cards in blackjack

You don't need to be a math genius: counting means adding or subtracting 1. Here's the Hi-Lo system, the most widely used in the world, in five steps.

Why card counting works

In blackjack, cards that have been dealt don't go back into the shoe until the shuffle. When lots of small cards are gone, there are proportionally more tens and aces left. That favors the player: more 3-to-2 blackjacks, more successful doubles, more dealer busts. Counting tells you when that happens so you can bet more.

Small cards: +1. Middle cards: 0. Big cards: −1. A full deck adds up to exactly 0, which makes it a "balanced" system.

Step 2: the running count

Start at 0 after the shuffle and add the value of every card you see. Example: 5, king, 3, 2, ace, 6 gives +1 −1 +1 +1 −1 +1 = +2.

Pro tip: count cards in pairs. A ten and a four cancel out, so you skip them at a glance. Target: a full deck in under 25 seconds.

Step 3: the true count

A +6 at the start of a six-deck shoe is diluted across a lot of cards; near the end it carries real weight. So you divide the running count by the number of decks still to be dealt.

To estimate decks left, look at the height of the discard tray. In the game, the tray fills up at real scale so you get used to it.

Step 4: spreading your bets

Each point of true count adds roughly 0.5% to the player's edge, so the player moves ahead around +1. A classic 1-to-8 spread:

The resulting edge is small (around 0.5 to 1%) and the swings are huge: the simulator shows it takes tens of thousands of hands for the edge to show up.

Step 5: index plays (deviations)

Some decisions change with the true count. The most valuable ones: take insurance at +3, stand on 16 vs 10 at 0 or higher, stand on 15 vs 10 at +4, double 11 vs ace at +1, split tens vs 6 at +4 and vs 5 at +5. The full Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 list is in the guide.

Is card counting legal?

Counting in your head isn't illegal: it's just paying attention. Casinos can still refuse a player they suspect of counting, and any device at the table is prohibited. Here you practice with play chips: the goal is to understand the game, not to make money.
