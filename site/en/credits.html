Privacy

Blackjack Play asks for no account and sets no cookies. Your settings, play-chip balance and personal stats are stored only in your browser (local storage) or in the desktop app's folder. You can export or erase them at any time.

To improve the game, the site anonymously counts page views and in-game activity (for example "one round played in coach mode"), along with the approximate city provided by the hosting provider. No IP address, identifier or personal game data is stored. Cloudflare Web Analytics audience measurement works without cookies.

The hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

Responsible gaming

Blackjack Play is a practice tool: no real money can be wagered, and chips have no value. Real blackjack is a game of chance where, over time, the casino keeps the edge for almost every player.

If you feel you're losing control of your gambling, free and confidential help exists: in the US, 1-800-GAMBLER; in the UK, GamCare, 0808 8020 133; in Australia, Gambling Help Online, 1800 858 858. Gambling is for adults only.

Notice

Blackjack Play is not affiliated with any casino or gambling operator. Casino names used in the presets (Las Vegas, Atlantic City…) only describe common rule sets.
